# KhetPulse - Smart Farming, Better Tomorrow

> **An AI-powered smart farming platform for crop disease detection, treatment recommendations, and smart farm management.**

---

## 📌 Problem Statement
Traditional crop disease diagnosis relies on manual expert visits, leading to delayed treatment and massive yield losses for farmers[cite: 2]. Farmers need an instant, accurate, and affordable solution to identify diseases or pests and apply the correct treatment[cite: 2].

- **Theme:** Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development[cite: 2]
- **Category:** Software[cite: 2]

---

## ✨ Key Features
- **AI Crop Disease Detection:** Instant leaf image diagnosis using CNN deep learning models[cite: 2].
- **Smart Prescriptions:** Precise fertilizer, pesticide, and treatment advice with estimated recovery times[cite: 2].
- **Farm Utility Dashboard:**
  - 🌤️ **Weather Forecast:** Local weather insights to plan sowing and harvesting[cite: 2].
  - 📈 **Live Crop Prices:** Real-time regional market rates[cite: 2].
  - 🛠️ **Nearby Tool Shops:** Location-based equipment and agri-store locator[cite: 2].
  - ⚡ **Electricity Availability:** Power supply status and outage alerts[cite: 2].
  - 🔌 **Remote Motor Control:** Turn irrigation motors ON/OFF remotely[cite: 2].
- **Multilingual & Voice Support:** Accessible in Marathi, Hindi, and English with voice assistance[cite: 2].
